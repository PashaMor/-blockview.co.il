## English (en-US) — optional secondary

**Short description** (≤80)
A 3D real-estate map for Israel. Tap a building, see the apartments inside.

**Full description**
BlockView is a new way to discover real estate in Israel. Instead of scrolling an endless list, you fly over the city in 3D, tap a building, and see exactly which apartments inside it are for sale or rent.

What you can do:
• Explore an interactive 3D city and tap any building
• See units for sale and for rent inside the building itself
• Filter by price, rooms, size and deal type
• Save apartments to favorites and follow buildings you're watching
• Contact the owner or agent directly

Listings come straight from owners and agents who post with us — we don't scrape other sites. Own a property? Publish it in minutes, right from the app.

Available in 6 languages (Hebrew, English, Arabic, Spanish, French, Russian) with full right-to-left support.

Free to use. A Pro subscription unlocks unlimited saves and follows.
